# hisbond

**Custom tool** (`tools/hisbond/`, not bundled with prosapia). **`action: update`**: annotates the table it ran against and mints no child. **`-t`, `-i/--input-column`, `--binder-chain` and `--target-chains` are required** (no honest default for any of them). CPU only: the builder forces `gpus_per_task = 0`, so `-g 0` is never needed. Runs on **both executors**: `modal_image.py` ships (`debian_slim` + `gemmi==0.7.5` + `numpy`), and on vib `SAPIA_ACTIVATE_HISBOND` in `.env` points at `activation/vib/prosapia_python.sh`. Resources: 1 CPU, 2 GiB, 20 min per task. **Point `-i` at a relaxed PDB (`-i pyrosetta_path`) for the roles**: only a file with hydrogens gives `role_basis == tautomer`.

It answers one question: **for each designed His centre, does the imidazole reach a polar atom of the target (or of the rest of its own binder), through which ring nitrogen, and is that contact one acid would break?** Campaign sequencing and shortlist rules live in `ph-switch`, the run loop in `running-a-step`, commissioning in `verifying-a-tool`.

## Premise, and when its numbers are meaningless

One structure file per design holding a **binder chain** and one or more **target chains**, plus `--centers`: a comma-separated list of **1-BASED POSITIONS ALONG THE BINDER CHAIN IN FILE ORDER** (`12,45`), not residue numbers and not indices. There is **no default**, and **no other tool's column name appears anywhere in this tool**. Per-design values arrive through prosapia's `{expr}` islands, written by the caller:

```
--centers '{atomiumph_centre1_idx1},{atomiumph_centre2_idx1}'
```

**One island resolves to exactly one integer and the commas between islands are literal template text** (`prosapia/utils/expr.py`), so a comma-joined cell cannot ride through a single island: name one column per centre. Resolution happens once per design at submit time, so a row whose column is NA (fewer centres than the template names) becomes an **error row**, never a silently shorter centre list.

**A POSITION IS NEVER INHERITED FROM AN ANCESTOR ROW — `--centers` does NOT use the ordinary lineage lookup.** prosapia's `ctx.lookup` returns the first NON-NULL value found anywhere up `parent_table`/`parent_name`, so on a lineage carrying `atomiumph_centre<k>_idx1` at two levels (a second upstream leaf, an unlabelled re-run, a merge that lifts the column onto more than one generation) a design whose own cell is NA would silently resolve to **another row's histidine** and collect `OK` with `centers_his_ok == centers_n`. hisbond instead stops the walk at the **first table whose COLUMNS hold the name** and takes that table's cell as is, NaN included, so a blank own cell raises and the design becomes a loud error row. A legitimate walk (the column is simply absent from this table, e.g. a Boltz grandchild of the atomiumph row) still works and is **recorded**: `hisbond_centers_lineage_depth` is `0` when every island came from the design's own table and the distance of the **deepest** island otherwise, on every row that carries a position list (NA when nothing resolved). **This is the opposite of what `{expr}` does for every other flag in this workspace** — the `--fixed-positions` hand-off below still has the ordinary fall-through and still needs its own guard.

**Every index this tool writes is that same 1-based position**, cell prefixes included, so `centre<k>_idx1` IS the join key into every `per_centre*` cell. Generators renumber (rfd3 and Boltz number every chain from 1), so `centers_his_ok`, `binder_chain_n_res` and `expect_binder_len` are the only things between three numbering systems and a confident wrong answer.

**The contact is a heavy-atom proxy.** No hydrogen enters the geometry, even in a relaxed PDB that has them, so a count from a CIF and one from a relaxed PDB are the same kind of number. A qualifying contact:

```
--min-dist <= dist(N, partner) <= --max-dist          (defaults 2.4 and 3.5 A)
AND angle( bisector(N), N->partner ) <= --max-angle   (default 60 deg)
```

`bisector(N)` is the external bisector of the imidazole ring angle at ND1 or NE2: the negative of the sum of the unit vectors from that N to its two ring neighbours (CG + CE1 for ND1; CD2 + CE1 for NE2), the direction its substituent (an H, or a lone pair) points in. **ND1 and NE2 are measured separately**; the merged columns are the OR of the two, and the per-nitrogen target columns (`_nd1` / `_ne2`) are always written (no flag). An "ND1 contact" is ND1 inside the window and the cone and nothing more: what it is comes from the roles below.

**Intra-binder contacts** use the same window, cone and floor, excluding the centre's whole residue and only the **backbone** (N, CA, C, O, OXT) of its i-1 / i+1 neighbours (their side chains are kept). Target and binder partner-atom sets are disjoint, so no contact is shared or double-counted, and the binder roles are read on exactly the contacts `n_centres_hb_binder` counts. This is what a CORE histidine needs: protonation breaking a bond inside the fold shows in no target column.

**The distance floor is load-bearing.** A predictor can put two heavy atoms closer than any hydrogen bond (an NE2 2.07 A from a Thr OG1, down to 0.7 A); without the floor such a clash passes the gate. A partner under `--min-dist` counts in neither the qualifying nor the distance-only count and is reported as `n_centres_too_close_*`; `min_dist_target` stays the unconditional minimum. **The floor is per nitrogen-atom pair**: an atom 2.2 A from ND1 and 3.0 A from NE2 is excluded for ND1 and can still qualify through NE2, so `n_centres_too_close_target >= 1` with `n_centres_hb_target >= 1` is a possible, meaningful row (read the per-nitrogen cells).

**Every qualifying contact also gets a ROLE**, from two ingredients that never change the contact definition:

1. **The tautomer, from hydrogens when the file has any.** An H/D within 1.2 A of ND1 or NE2, found by distance and not by name, **belonging to the centre's own residue** (matched on `(chain, resnum, icode)`), marks that nitrogen as the donor; the other is the acceptor. An H of any other residue in that radius is counted (`n_centres_foreign_h`) and discarded. `centre<k>_tautomer` is `HD1` / `HE2` / `HD1+HE2` / `none`, and NA when the file has no hydrogen at all. **`none` (hydrogens in the file, none on this ring) is a broken reading, not a state**: that centre falls back to rule 2 and is counted in `n_centres_no_ring_h`, never read as two acceptors. **`HD1+HE2` (HIP) is real and kept**: both nitrogens are donors, so that centre has no acceptor role and every donor-only partner there is incoherent (`n_centres_hip`). The names `HD1`/`HE2` are only a cross-check: a disagreement is a stderr warning and changes no number.
2. **The partner class, from residue and atom name** (a standard-rules table, `PARTNER_CLASSES` in `hisbond_worker.py`, not a calculation). Donor-only: backbone `N` except PRO, LYS `NZ`, ARG `NE`/`NH1`/`NH2`, ASN `ND2`, GLN `NE2`, TRP `NE1`. Acceptor-only: backbone `O`/`OXT`, ASP `OD1`/`OD2`, GLU `OE1`/`OE2`, ASN `OD1`, GLN `OE1`. Both-capable: SER `OG`, THR `OG1`, TYR `OH`, HIS `ND1`/`NE2`. Everything else, PRO `N` included, is unclassified. **The NEUTRAL histidine spellings (`HID`, `HIE`, `HSD`, `HSE`) are canonicalised to `HIS` before this lookup**, so a His partner in a relaxed PDB that writes variant names is both-capable and not unclassified. **`HIP`/`HSP` are NOT folded in**: a doubly protonated ring has an H on each nitrogen and no lone pair, so it has its own **donor-only** entries for ND1 and NE2. Backbone `N`/`O` of any of them still reach the `('*', atom)` rules. The distinction matters at a centre's protonated nitrogen, where a both-capable partner scores `don` and a donor-only one scores `incoh` — and `incoh == 0` is what the clean gates require, so folding HIP in would hide an incoherence and read **false-clean, never false-dirty**.

**Why the acceptor role is the pH-sensitive one.** Acid protonates the lone-pair nitrogen, the one without an H. A contact where the His N **accepts** from a donor is the one protonation breaks (donor-donor repulsion plus the new charge); one where the His N-H **donates** to an acceptor survives. `n_centres_switch_candidate` counts centres with at least one acceptor-role contact and zero donor-role contacts; `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean` also requires zero incoherent contacts at that centre, and **is the one to gate on**, because an incoherence at the lone-pair nitrogen argues the tautomer is the other way round and would invert the verdict. **This is the direction bind at neutral pH, release in acid**; for a design meant to bind in acid the reading is the mirror image and these columns are the wrong way round.

**Position columns.** hisbond writes `centre<k>_idx1` (the k-th supplied position, read back off the residue the tool found there: the 1..L numbering atomium and ProteinMPNN impose) and `centre<k>_resnum` (its `auth_seq_id`), so a later `--fixed-positions` island has a number to consume. They are positions in the structure that was read, not in the designed sequence. **`centre<k>_idx1` is the k-th value of `--centers` in every execution**, so it is not itself a check: what makes it trustworthy is the His-name check plus `--expect-binder-len-col`. **Pass that flag** if you will use these positions.

**Scope: histidine centres only.** The residue found at each supplied position must be a histidine, read **off the structure** -- that identity check is the whole reason positions are given rather than histidines auto-discovered, and it is the only thing standing between a wrong position and a plausible number. There is no token vocabulary any more: an atomiumph run with `--center-mode acid_p` / `all_p` pins Asp/Glu, so **every row still errors here by design**, now because the residue is ASP/GLU rather than because a token was not a His token. **New signature:** `error: centre position 12 of binder chain D is ASP D12, not a histidine -- either the positions do not map onto this structure, or they are not His centres at all (an acid_p / all_p design pins Asp/Glu and is out of scope here)`.

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A centre's residue is not a histidine | **The row ERRORS** (`error: centre position N of binder chain D is LEU D12, not a histidine ...`), every science column NA. Never a 0. |
| `--binder-chain` is a chain that exists but is the wrong molecule | It runs. If the residue at the centre position is not His it errors; **if it happens to be His it returns plausible numbers for the wrong chain.** `--expect-binder-len-col` is the defence. |
| Binder-only input (no target chains) | The named target chains are absent, so **the row errors**. A target chain with no N/O atoms also errors. |
| The input is a designed complex, not a prediction | It runs and measures whether the designer's own rotamer reaches the target. Keep it apart with `-l`. |

**It is not a pH measurement** (nothing about pKa, protonated state or whether a contact is lost in acid; the pKa is `propka`, the design-side claim is `atomiumph_ddE_mean`). **It is not an interface metric**: area and complementarity are `cms`, interface energy `pyrosetta` (`if_dG`, `if_dSASA`, `if_hbonds`), footprint and hotspot recall `epitope`. **It scores the coordinates it is given**: on a prediction it is only as good as the prediction, so read it after the pose gate (`boltzconf`, `usalign` against the designed backbone).

## Invocation and flags

```bash
sapia run hisbond <run_dir> -t <table> -i boltz_cof_path \
    --binder-chain D --target-chains A,B,C \
    --centers '{atomiumph_centre1_idx1},{atomiumph_centre2_idx1}' \
    --expect-binder-len-col atomiumph_seq_len -l cof

sapia collect hisbond <run_dir> -t <table> -l cof
```

**`collect` needs the same `-l` as the run**: the label is part of the column leaf (`hisbond_cof_status`), and without it collect looks in `.../hisbond/`, finds nothing and reports every design missing. To get roles from the tautomer, run once on the relaxed PDB and once on the AF3/Boltz CIF under different `-l`: the contact geometry is the same kind of number, `role_basis` says which evidence each used.

| Flag | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `-i/--input-column` | **none, required** | The structure column of the **predicted complex** (binder + target), resolved up the lineage. A binder-only structure submits but is meaningless (it errors per row). |
| `--binder-chain` | **none, required** | Exactly one chain. Centre positions are counted along it, in file order. |
| `--target-chains` | **none, required** | Chain mini-language (`,` separates, `:` is an inclusive range): `A,B,C`, `A:C`. Disjoint from `--binder-chain` (checked at submit). A partner is every N/O heavy atom of these chains in a residue that is not a tabulated non-amino-acid; an untabulated residue name passes. |
| `--centers` | **none, required** | Comma-separated **1-based** binder positions (`12,45`). Per-design values go in as `{expr}` islands, one island per centre: `'{atomiumph_centre1_idx1},{atomiumph_centre2_idx1}'`. Positions must be `>= 1` and distinct (both refused at submit and in the worker). A design whose island resolves to nothing or to a non-integer is an **error row**; if EVERY design fails, the submit is refused instead. Echoed verbatim as `hisbond_centers_arg`, resolved per design as `hisbond_centers_spec`. |
| `--expect-binder-len-col` | **none, and that is the wrong default** | Column holding the expected binder length: an integer (a sequence-length column) or a plain one-letter sequence (a sequence column). No column name is assumed. **Pass it whenever such a column exists**, since the positions are counted along `--binder-chain` and a wrong chain puts every one of them on the wrong residue. A disagreement is an error row, and a column that resolves nowhere is also an error row (a check asked for and impossible is never skipped). |
| `--min-dist` | `2.4` A | Distance floor; `>= 0` and below `--max-dist` (an empty window is refused at submit). |
| `--max-dist` | `3.5` A | Donor/acceptor heavy-atom bar. |
| `--max-angle` | `60.0` deg | Orientation proxy, in (0, 180]; **`180` makes it a pure distance test** (then `n_partner_atoms == n_near_target`). |
| `--env-radius` | `6.0` A | Radius around each centre's imidazole ring centroid (mean of CG, ND1, CD2, CE1, NE2) in which charged **target** atoms are counted into `centre<k>_n_acidic_env` (ASP `OD1`/`OD2`, GLU `OE1`/`OE2`) and `centre<k>_n_basic_env` (LYS `NZ`, ARG `NE`/`NH1`/`NH2`). `> 0` (refused at submit and in the worker). **ATOMS, not residues** (an Asp is 2, an Arg 3). pKa context, never a pKa. Target only: the binder's own charges are in the free binder too. |
| `--designs-per-task` | `20` | The work is milliseconds per design. |

**Partner-atom rules.** Partners are **N and O only**; sulfur (Met SD, Cys SG) is excluded (weak, long acceptor; the 3.5 A bar would be wrong). Waters, ions and tabulated ligands are excluded on **both** sides (`target_n_het_atoms` counts what was dropped). Chain IDs and residue numbers are the `auth_asym_id` / `auth_seq_id` namespace (`--binder-chain` means the `auth` letter). A partner atom reached by both nitrogens is one atom in `n_partner_atoms` inside `per_centre` and appears in both per-nitrogen cells. **Centre-level counts count centres, not atoms**: a centre reaching one partner through ND1 and another through NE2 counts once in the merged column and once in each per-nitrogen column, so **expect `nd1 + ne2 > n_centres_hb_target` on real batches at any `--max-angle`**; only `max(nd1, ne2) <= merged <= nd1 + ne2` is guaranteed. **Never compute an NE2-only count as `merged - nd1`** (it can go negative): count centres whose `per_centre_ne2_target` entry is not `:none` and whose `per_centre_nd1_target` entry for the same index is. One atom qualifying through both nitrogens of one centre is not expected geometrically (bisectors diverge ~125 deg); if a real file does it, report it as a finding.

## Trust columns: read these before the science

| Column | Expected | How to read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hisbond_status` | `OK` | The only proof of success. `error: ...` rows keep the echo and the trust facts established before the failure; every science column is NA. |
| `hisbond_centers_his_ok` | **equals `hisbond_centers_n`** | **Read first.** On an `OK` row it always equals `centers_n` (a disagreement fails the design), so the real check is that the row is `OK`. On an error row it is how many centres passed before the failing one. |
| `hisbond_centers_n` | the number of positions you wrote in `--centers` | How many positions were SUPPLIED for this design, not how many were found. It is the denominator of every `n_centres_*` column. Compare with the centre count of whatever produced the positions (e.g. `atomiumph_n_centers`); a mismatch means the islands and that row's centres came from different runs. |
| `hisbond_centers_arg` | the `--centers` string you typed | Echo of the template verbatim, braces included (`{atomiumph_centre1_idx1},{atomiumph_centre2_idx1}`): **the provenance of every position on the row**, and the replacement for the old `centers_col`. Identical on every row of a leaf. Kept on error rows. |
| `hisbond_centers_spec` | e.g. `12,45` | This design's RESOLVED positions, comma-joined, **every one of them including those past the `centre<k>_*` cap**. Set before ANY other check (the structure included), so it survives every error row that got as far as the task -- read it to see which positions a failing row was asked about. |
| `hisbond_centers_lineage_depth` | **`0`** on a table that carries the centre columns itself | **Where the positions came from**, on every row that carries a position list. `0` = every island resolved from the design's OWN table and is exact. `> 0` = **the DEEPEST island** resolved that many steps up — a template naming two producers can have islands at different depths and only the deepest is reported (normal when hisbond runs on a Boltz/AF3 grandchild, whose table has no centre columns). **The positions are never inherited to FILL A BLANK** -- the walk stops at the first table that HAS the column -- so a depth > 0 means the whole column was absent here, not that one cell was patched. Still check the lineage is one producer row per branch. NA when the positions could not be resolved at all. |
| `hisbond_binder_chain_n_res` | the binder length you expect | Residues in `--binder-chain`, in file order: **what a centre position counts along.** A target-length value (152 rather than 44) means the chain letter is the wrong molecule. |
| `hisbond_expect_binder_len` | = `binder_chain_n_res` | NA when `--expect-binder-len-col` was not used, and then nothing rules out a wrong chain letter. |
| `hisbond_binder_chain` / `_target_chains` / `_chain_ids_found` | echo | Which molecule these numbers describe; `chain_ids_found` lists every chain in file order, what to read when a chain is reported absent. Kept on error rows. |
| `hisbond_target_chain_n_res` | e.g. 3 x the protomer length for a trimer | Amino-acid residues of the target chains. Catches a half-present target or one protomer named where three were meant. |
| `hisbond_target_n_het_atoms` | `0` | Target atoms the het filter dropped. Non-zero: the target carried a ligand or water that this tool ignored, so a His bonding it reads as no contact. |
| `hisbond_plddt_scale` | `0-100` on Boltz/AF3 CIF; `unknown` on a relaxed PyRosetta PDB (B-factors all 0) | `0-100`, `0-1` or `unknown`, inferred from the largest B-factor in the whole file. **Read before `his_plddt_min`.** |
| `hisbond_structure_has_hydrogens` | `False` on a prediction, `True` on a relaxed PDB | True if any H/D atom is present. Hydrogens are read for one thing only: which ring N carries one. |
| `hisbond_role_basis` | `tautomer` on a relaxed PDB, `partner_class` on a CIF | **Read before any role column.** `tautomer`: every centre had a hydrogen reading. `partner_class`: none did, so roles rest on the partner's chemistry and a both-capable partner is `amb`, not resolved. **`mixed`: some centres did and some did not (reachable: a file with hydrogens can hold a His with none on its ring); it is two kinds of answer in one row, so do not pool it with either pure mode.** Never average a `tautomer` batch with a `partner_class` batch. **`partner_class` is ambiguous on its own** (no hydrogens in the file, or hydrogens and every centre read `none`): read the pair `structure_has_hydrogens` + `n_centres_no_ring_h`, `False` + `0` being the honest hydrogen-free reading and `True` + non-zero a file to go and look at. NA on an error row. |
| `hisbond_n_centres_no_ring_h` | `0` | Centres in a hydrogen-bearing file with no own-residue H within 1.2 A of ND1 or NE2. A neutral His always carries a ring H, so this is a broken reading: **non-zero means exclude the row from a pH shortlist and look at the file.** Row total (covers centres past the 8-column cap). |
| `hisbond_n_centres_hip` | `0` at neutral pH | Centres read as `HD1+HE2`. A HIP centre has no acceptor role, so a batch with HIP raises `n_contacts_incoherent_target` for reasons about the relaxer's protonation choice, not the designs: report the two together. Row total. |
| `hisbond_n_centres_foreign_h` | `0` | Centres with another residue's H (neighbour, water) within 1.2 A of a ring N. Never used for the tautomer; a clash detector. Row total. |
| `hisbond_n_contacts_incoherent_target` | `0` or a handful | Qualifying contacts whose His N and partner are the same kind (acceptor-acceptor or donor-donor): impossible, counted and given no role. Only the `tautomer` mode can produce one. A large share of all contacts means the tautomers or the chain mapping are not to be trusted, not that the design is bad: report the fraction. |
| `hisbond_centre1_tautomer` ... `centre8_tautomer` | `HD1` or `HE2` on a relaxed PDB; NA on a CIF | Per reported centre, in `--centers` order. `none` means that centre's roles came from the partner class (see `n_centres_no_ring_h`). NA past the row's own centre count and on error rows. A NA on an OK row means "no reading"; `none` is the value that means no ring hydrogen. |
| `hisbond_min_dist` / `_max_dist` / `_max_angle` / `_env_radius` | `2.4` / `3.5` / `60.0` / `6.0` | Echo of the question. A count is comparable only at the same cutoffs; do not pool leaves that differ. |
| `hisbond_centres_idx1_eq_resnum` | `1` on AF3 / Boltz output | **Read before passing `centre<k>_resnum` to anything.** `1` when `idx1 == resnum` for every reported centre (binder numbered contiguously from 1); `0` means a build from `resnum` would pin the wrong residue and `idx1` is the one to use. Also `0` (not an error) when a residue number was unreadable. |
| `hisbond_centres_reported` | equals `hisbond_centers_n` | `min(centers_n, 8)`: how many centres got a `centre<k>_*` set. Below `centers_n` means more centres than columns (**the cap is 8**, each centre costing five columns); every other column, and `centers_spec`, still covers all of them. |

## Column spellings: copy the names

There is no rule; both spellings exist in this one tool.

| Family | Spelling | Names |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Centre-list | American `center...` | `centers_n`, `centers_his_ok`, `centers_arg`, `centers_spec`, `centers_lineage_depth` |
| Per-centre counts and cells | British `centre...` | `n_centres_hb_target` (and `_nd1`/`_ne2`/`_binder`), `n_centres_polar_dist_only_target`, `n_centres_too_close_target/_binder`, `n_centres_acceptor_role_target`, `n_centres_donor_role_target`, `n_centres_switch_candidate`, `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean`, `n_centres_no_ring_h`, `n_centres_hip`, `n_centres_foreign_h`, `per_centre`, `centres_reported`, `centres_idx1_eq_resnum`, `centre1_idx1`..`centre8_idx1`, `centre1_resnum`..`centre8_resnum`, `centre1_tautomer`..`centre8_tautomer`, `centre1_n_acidic_env` / `_n_basic_env`..`centre8_*` |
| Contact totals | `n_contacts_` (plural), with `_target` or `_binder` | `n_contacts_incoherent_target`, `n_contacts_ambiguous_target`, `n_contacts_unclassified_target`, `n_contacts_*_binder`, `n_contacts_binder_entries` |
| Defect counters | `n_centres_`, **no `_target` suffix** | `n_centres_no_ring_h`, `n_centres_hip`, `n_centres_foreign_h`; likewise the boolean `any_switch_candidate` |
| Per-nitrogen counts | nitrogen **last**, lower case | `n_centres_hb_target_nd1`, `any_target_hb_proxy_nd1`, `min_dist_target_nd1` |
| Per-nitrogen cells | nitrogen **in the middle**, `per_centre` **singular** | `per_centre_nd1_target`, `per_centre_ne2_target`, `per_centre_nd1_binder`, `per_centre_ne2_binder` |

All of these are `KeyError`: `n_centres_his_ok`, `centers_hb_target`, `n_centres_incoherent_target`, `n_contacts_no_ring_h`, `any_switch_candidate_target`, `per_centres_nd1`, `per_centre_target_nd1`, `n_centres_hb_nd1_target`, any upper-case `ND1`. A comparison such as `centres_reported` against `centers_n` mixes the two spellings in one expression: write it out.

## What it collects

All columns are leaf-prefixed `hisbond_` (`hisbond_<label>_` with `-l`). On disk: one `<name>.tsv` per design under `<run_dir>/<table>/hisbond[_<label>]/`, plus sub-manifests in `hisbond_tasks/`. Trust columns are in the table above.

| Column | Meaning | How to read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `per_centre` | One `;`-joined entry per centre, in `--centers` order: `<idx1>:<RESNAME>:target_min=<A>:n_partner_atoms=<n>:binder_min=<A>:n_partner_atoms_binder=<n>:n_near_target=<n>:n_near_binder=<n>:n_too_close_target=<n>:n_too_close_binder=<n>` | The whole measurement per centre. `n_partner_atoms` = unique partner ATOMS (not residues) passing window and cone; `n_near_*` = the same atoms on the window alone, so **the gap is the cost of the proxy**; `n_too_close_*` = atoms under the floor, in neither count. `NA` in a `*_min` field: that side had no N/O atom. |
| `n_centres_hb_target` | Centres with >= 1 qualifying **target** contact | The count behind the gate. `0` is a measurement; NA is an error row. |
| `any_target_hb_proxy` | `n_centres_hb_target >= 1` | **THE CONTACT GATE COLUMN.** Bool with NA: read with `_flag`, never `.astype(bool)`. |
| `min_dist_target` | Smallest ND1/NE2-to-target N/O distance over all centres, A. **Unconditional** (ignores window and cone) | Ranks near misses (2.6 vs 4.5 A). **Below ~2.4 A is a clash the floor already excluded, never evidence of a bond.** Equals `min(nd1, ne2)`. NA when the target had no N/O atom, never 0. |
| `n_centres_hb_target_nd1` / `_ne2` | Centres with >= 1 qualifying target contact through that nitrogen | Each `<=` merged; the ND1-vs-NE2 rate is `(nd1 >= 1).mean()` against `(ne2 >= 1).mean()`, and the two overlap. |
| `any_target_hb_proxy_nd1` / `_ne2` | `n_centres_hb_target_<n> >= 1` | **THE ND1 / NE2 GATE COLUMNS.** Bool with NA, `_flag`. No claim about which atom donates. A design can be True on both. |
| `min_dist_target_nd1` / `_ne2` | The unconditional minimum from that nitrogen | Which nitrogen faces the target even when neither qualifies (`nd1 = 3.1`, `ne2 = 7.8` is an imidazole turned ND1-first, a near miss). |
| `per_centre_nd1_target` / `_ne2_target` | Qualifying target atoms through that nitrogen, `;`-joined: `<idx1>:<chain>:<resnum>:<resname>:<atom>=<dist>/<angle>` | The partner identity. **`<idx1>` is the centre's 1-BASED binder position, i.e. `centre<k>_idx1` itself** -- that is the join key, with no arithmetic. Every entry carries its own position (one centre with three atoms gives three entries), and a centre with none is `<idx1>:none`: **group by the leading number, and never assume one entry per centre**. Resnum carries any insertion code (`112A`), `?` when absent; chain and number are `auth_*` (`label_*` if the file has none). `<angle>` is degrees off that nitrogen's bisector. Example `6:A:112:ASP:OD1=2.85/31.2;11:none` for `--centers '6,11'`. An atom qualifying through both nitrogens appears in both. |
| `n_centres_polar_dist_only_target` | Centres with >= 1 target N/O in the window, ignoring orientation | Always `>= n_centres_hb_target`; the difference is what the cone removed. |
| `n_centres_too_close_target` / `_binder` | Centres with >= 1 partner under `--min-dist` | A predictor clash, never a bond. It must not veto the design (another centre can be good) and does not veto the centre (per-pair floor). |
| `per_centre_role_target` | Per centre: `<idx1>:acc=<n>:don=<n>:amb=<n>:incoh=<n>:unclass=<n>` | What the contacts ARE. The five counts partition that centre's qualifying target contact ENTRIES across both nitrogens (sum = the `=`-bearing entries it contributes to the two per-nitrogen cells; a `:none` placeholder adds 0). `acc` = the His N accepted (pH-sensitive); `don` = its N-H donated; `amb` = `partner_class` mode with a both-capable partner; `incoh` = two donors or two acceptors, **no role given**; `unclass` = a partner the rules table does not name, **no role given**. |
| `n_centres_acceptor_role_target` / `_donor_role_target` | Centres with >= 1 acceptor-role / donor-role target contact | They **overlap** (one centre can do both and is then no candidate) and do not partition `n_centres_hb_target`. |
| `n_centres_switch_candidate` | Centres with >= 1 acceptor-role contact **and zero** donor-role contacts | Ambiguous, incoherent and unclassified contacts do not decide it. A centre with only ambiguous contacts counts 0. |
| `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean` | The same, **and zero incoherent contacts** at that centre | **GATE ON THIS ONE.** `clean <= n_centres_switch_candidate`. |
| `any_switch_candidate` | `n_centres_switch_candidate >= 1` | Bool with NA, `_flag`. No `_target` suffix. Built on the bare count, so it is the looser headline form. |
| `n_contacts_incoherent_target` / `_ambiguous_target` / `_unclassified_target` | Row totals of contacts carrying no role | Incoherent: tautomer mode only (0 in `partner_class` mode is a tautology). Ambiguous: only `partner_class` mode or a `no_ring_h` centre; large relative to `acc + don` means the leaf answered little. Unclassified: a partner the rules table does not name (PRO `N`, a modified residue, an untabulated ligand atom); look at the partner names. |
| `n_centres_no_ring_h` / `_hip` / `_foreign_h` | Row totals of reading defects | Expected `0 / 0 / 0`. See the trust table. |
| `n_centres_hb_binder` / `min_dist_binder` | Centres with >= 1 qualifying contact to the rest of the binder; the minimum on that side | A His satisfied by its own chain is not available to the target. High here with `n_centres_hb_target == 0` is "the switch points inwards". |
| `per_centre_nd1_binder` / `_ne2_binder` | Qualifying intra-binder entries, same format as the target twins (same 1-based `<idx1>` prefix), `<idx1>:none` when none | **An atom name ending in `*` means the partner residue is itself one of the row's designed centres** (two centres bonded to each other); the mark never changes the role. Without these cells a Ser OG, a nearby backbone O and a designed His are the same number. |
| `per_centre_role_binder` | Per centre, same five counts as the target twin | The five counts partition that centre's qualifying binder ENTRIES. |
| `n_centres_acceptor_role_binder` / `_donor_role_binder` | Centres with >= 1 acceptor-role / donor-role intra-binder contact | Acceptor: the lone-pair N accepts from a binder donor, the bond protonation breaks. Donor: survives. They overlap. |
| `n_centres_switch_candidate_binder` | **LOOSE**: centres with acc >= 1 and don == 0 on the binder side | Incoherent contacts allowed. |
| `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_binder` | **STRICT**: the same, also incoh == 0 at that centre | The binder-side shortlist column. |
| `any_switch_candidate_binder` | Bool, **loose**: `n_centres_switch_candidate_binder >= 1` | Bool with NA, `_flag`. |
| `n_contacts_incoherent_binder` / `_ambiguous_binder` / `_unclassified_binder` | Binder ENTRIES with same-kind partners / a both-capable partner and no tautomer / a partner in no rule | Incoherent is only reachable with a tautomer; `== 0` is a tautology in `partner_class` mode. |
| `n_contacts_binder_entries` | All qualifying binder ENTRIES over every centre, counted per nitrogen (an atom both nitrogens reach counts twice) | Denominator of the partition: the five counts of `per_centre_role_binder`, summed over the row, equal it. |
| `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_any` | **STRICT across BOTH sides**: centres with acc >= 1 on either side, don == 0 **and** incoh == 0 on both | For a His that may bond the target, inside the fold, or both. |
| `any_switch_candidate_clean_any` | Bool: `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_any >= 1` | **Not `any_switch_candidate` OR-ed with `any_switch_candidate_binder`**: see below. |
| `centre<k>_idx1`, k = 1..8 | The k-th position of `--centers`, read back off the residue found there (1-based along the binder chain in file order) | **The column a downstream `--fixed-positions` island wants, and the join key into every `per_centre*` cell.** NA past the row's centre count (an absent centre, never position 0) and on every error row. `centre1` is the first value of `--centers`, not the N-terminal-most His. |
| `centre<k>_resnum` | The same residue's `auth_seq_id` | For a viewer or a selection **against that same file**; any insertion code is dropped (`112A` -> `112`). NA when the file gave no integer. **Do not pass it to atomium unless `centres_idx1_eq_resnum == 1`.** |
| `centre<k>_n_acidic_env` / `_n_basic_env` | Charged target atoms (ASP/GLU, LYS/ARG) within `env_radius` of the ring centroid | pKa **context**: acid around a His raises its pKa, base lowers it; a centre buried among Lys/Arg disfavours its protonated form. ATOMS, not residues. `0` is a measurement; NA is past the centre count or an error row. |
| `his_plddt_min` | Smallest B-factor over the side-chain atoms of the centre His residues | The predictor's own confidence in the side chains the measurement rests on. Low means the rotamer is a guess and so is every distance. NA (never 0.0) when `plddt_scale` is `unknown`. Typical batch medians sit around 55-70 on `0-100`; set a floor from the batch. |
| `hisbond_path` | The structure that was scored; `""` on an error row | Framework-stamped. |

**LOOSE vs CLEAN vs PER-SIDE: the three booleans do not mean the same kind of thing.** `any_switch_candidate` (target side) and `any_switch_candidate_binder` are **LOOSE**: some centre has >= 1 acceptor-role and zero donor-role contacts **on that side**, and incoherent contacts are allowed; their counters are `n_centres_switch_candidate` and `n_centres_switch_candidate_binder`. The **STRICT** per-side counters add incoh == 0: `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean` and `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_binder`. `any_switch_candidate_clean_any` is **STRICT and spans both sides at once**: zero donor-role and zero incoherent contacts anywhere, counter `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_any`. **It is therefore not the OR of the two per-side booleans**: it is False where both are True (one centre accepting from the target while another, or the same one, donates inside the binder), and True where neither is (a centre reaching the bar only by pooling an acceptor-role contact on one side with silence on the other). Pick the one whose question you are asking and say which in the report.

**The five role counts partition ENTRIES, not centres.** As row columns, `acc` and `don` count CENTRES (`n_centres_acceptor_role_*`) while `incoh` / `amb` / `unclass` count ENTRIES (`n_contacts_*`), so the row-level five sum to nothing. The partition holds per centre, inside `per_centre_role_target` / `per_centre_role_binder`, and `n_contacts_binder_entries` is the binder row denominator. **The accounting identity, the first thing to check**: `sum(acc) + sum(don)` over `per_centre_role_target` plus `n_contacts_incoherent_target + n_contacts_ambiguous_target + n_contacts_unclassified_target` equals the row's qualifying target entries; a mismatch means the role reading and the contact reading disagree about what a contact is.

**NA is never 0, and an empty status is a third thing.**

| Situation | `hisbond_status` | science columns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Success | `OK` | all filled |
| Per-design refusal: a centre whose residue is not a His (`error: centre position 12 of binder chain D is ASP D12, not a histidine ...` -- this is how an Asp/Glu table errors); a centre position past the chain end; a position below 1 or repeated; a binder length disagreeing with `--expect-binder-len-col` (or that check unresolvable for the row); a named chain absent (`error: binder chain Z absent from x.cif (have: A,B,C,D)`); target chains with no N/O atom; **an incomplete imidazole** (a His missing CD2 or any ring atom errors the WHOLE design, even where ND1 alone was computable); an unparsable position list | `error: ...` | NA; echo and trust columns kept |
| Structure column unresolvable, or a `--centers` island that resolved to nothing / to a non-integer for this design (the commonest cause: the row has fewer centres than the template names); file missing or unreadable | `error: could not resolve ...` / `error: structure missing: /path` | NA |
| Worker crashed / no per-design TSV | `error: worker crashed` / `missing` | NA |
| **The table HAS the structure column and this row's own cell is blank** | the task writes `error: could not resolve ...` but collect skips the row (blank input cells are dropped), so the status stays **empty** | NA |

On an error row the role, environment and position columns are NA too (`role_basis` NA, not `partner_class`), including the row whose centre is not a His. On an `OK` row, `centre<k>_*` beyond `centres_reported` is NA for an absent centre, never 0. **Compare the count of non-empty `hisbond_status` values with the row count.** The incomplete-imidazole refusal is deliberate (returning only the ND1 half would hide a missing NE2 companion): report the count of such rows (`hisbond_status` starting `error: histidine ... has no`), do not filter them away.

**`-f/--filter` is a run flag only; collect has none.** Rows the filter excluded at submit collect as `missing` with NA columns: with N rows and a filter keeping k, N-k `missing` rows are expected ("filtered out"); treat `missing` as a problem only for rows the filter kept.

## Reading the gates

- **The contact gate** is boolean, with no published threshold: `hisbond_status == "OK" and hisbond_any_target_hb_proxy`. For "every His must carry the switch" gate on `n_centres_hb_target == centers_n` instead and expect far fewer survivors.
- **Which role: the gate a pH switch wants** is `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean >= 1` together with `n_centres_no_ring_h == 0` (`any_switch_candidate` is the looser headline). It supersedes the ND1-only gate: ND1-vs-NE2 was a proxy for "which nitrogen is the lone-pair one", and the tautomer answers that directly. On a `partner_class` row it is a weaker claim (ambiguous contacts were set aside, not resolved), so **report `role_basis` next to the survivor count, every time**. Before quoting a survivor count also state `role_basis` counts, the `structure_has_hydrogens` + `n_centres_no_ring_h` pair, and the totals of `n_centres_hip`, `n_centres_foreign_h`, incoherent, ambiguous and unclassified; a non-zero HIP total explains part of the incoherent total on its own.
- **Which nitrogen.** For a switch meant to work through ND1 specifically, gate on `any_target_hb_proxy_nd1` and report the ND1-vs-NE2 rate over the batch; they are not exclusive, so rates do not sum to the merged one. The split is geometric: read the tautomer warning before reading it as "ND1 donates".
- **How much to trust the cone.** If `n_centres_polar_dist_only_target` exceeds `n_centres_hb_target` on most of a batch, say so: a distance-only pass the bisector rejects is a polar atom beside the imidazole, not in front of it (for example a backbone O at 2.56 A and 86 deg off the bisector). A distance-only screen over-reports binding substantially.
- **Whether the side chain is worth reading.** `his_plddt_min` low makes the geometry a guess.
- **An intra-binder switch call says that protonating that His breaks that bond, not when.** At a buried centre with a depressed pKa it happens only well below the release pH (typical candidate centres: complex pKa ~1.5-5, free pKa ~3.5-5.5; at pH 6.5 a centre of pKa 4 is ~0.3 % protonated). Read the flag together with `propka_*_centre<k>_pka_free` / `_pka_complex` (geometry says a breakable bond exists, propka says at what pH it goes) and use propka's `buried_free` to decide whether the centre is in the core at all; nothing here computes burial. A backbone O at the lone-pair N scores `incoh` in tautomer mode and could empty the strict gates, so check `n_contacts_incoherent_binder` before concluding a strict gate is honest on a new batch. Typical for relaxed core designs in tautomer mode: loose and strict binder gates agree (incoherent entries are rare), most acceptor-role binder entries have a **designed-His partner** (the `*` mark), a minority a side-chain donor and none a backbone N, and interface-placed designs make almost no intra-binder bonds.

## Filters a caller can write

**The leaf prefix is `hisbond_` only when the run used no `-l`**; with `-l cof` every column is `hisbond_cof_*` and a pasted filter raises `KeyError`. Set `L` once.

```python
L = "hisbond"          # a leaf run with `-l cof` is L = "hisbond_cof"


def _flag(df, col, *, na):
    # Bool-with-NA as a real mask. After the TSV round trip such a column can be object
    # dtype holding the STRINGS "True"/"False", and bool("False") is True.
    raw = df[col]
    text = raw.astype(str).str.strip().str.lower()
    return text.eq("true").where(~raw.isna() & text.ne("nan"), na)


def apply_filter(df):
    # THE CONTACT GATE with the mapping trusted. NA fails every clause.
    ok = df[f"{L}_status"] == "OK"
    mapped = df[f"{L}_centers_his_ok"] == df[f"{L}_centers_n"]
    # Tool's own echo of the expected length: NA on both sides without
    # --expect-binder-len-col, and NaN != NaN, so this admits nothing then.
    right_chain = df[f"{L}_binder_chain_n_res"] == df[f"{L}_expect_binder_len"]
    bonded = _flag(df, f"{L}_any_target_hb_proxy", na=False)      # or ..._nd1
    scale_ok = df[f"{L}_plddt_scale"] == "0-100"
    confident = scale_ok & (df[f"{L}_his_plddt_min"].fillna(-1) >= 70)   # floor from the batch
    return df[ok & mapped & right_chain & bonded & confident]
```

**The pH-switch gate** replaces the contact gate for a pH campaign. **The confidence clause belongs to the PREDICTION leaf, never to the relaxed one**: a relaxed PDB has no pLDDT (`plddt_scale == "unknown"`, `his_plddt_min` NA on every row), so the clause is False everywhere and the filter returns an empty frame that reads as "no design passed" but is a property of the B-factor column. Take roles from the relaxed leaf `R` and confidence from the prediction leaf `P`, and **check both leaf names against the table header** (an unlabelled leaf is plain `hisbond`; a wrong literal is a `KeyError` if you are lucky and the other leaf's numbers if not). If only a relaxed leaf exists, drop the confidence clause and say so.

```python
def apply_filter(df):
    R, P = "hisbond_relax", "hisbond"       # roles/tautomers leaf, pose-confidence leaf
    ok = df[f"{R}_status"] == "OK"
    mapped = df[f"{R}_centers_his_ok"] == df[f"{R}_centers_n"]
    right_chain = df[f"{R}_binder_chain_n_res"] == df[f"{R}_expect_binder_len"]
    # >= 1 centre accepts, donates nowhere, no incoherent contact (fillna: NA must FAIL)
    switch = df[f"{R}_n_centres_switch_candidate_clean"].fillna(0) >= 1
    # a no-ring-H centre has no tautomer: not-yet-answered, exclude and count them
    read_ok = df[f"{R}_n_centres_no_ring_h"].fillna(-1) == 0
    confident = (df[f"{P}_plddt_scale"] == "0-100") & (df[f"{P}_his_plddt_min"].fillna(-1) >= 70)
    return df[ok & mapped & right_chain & switch & read_ok & confident]
```

Do not require `n_centres_too_close_target == 0` by default: a clash on one centre must not veto a design whose other centre is clean (add it only to demand no clash anywhere, and say so). The `70` is a property of the campaign, not of the table: take it from the batch's `his_plddt_min` distribution and record it. To see what the cone cost, select `df[f"{L}_n_centres_polar_dist_only_target"] > df[f"{L}_n_centres_hb_target"]`.

## Handing the centres to atomium

The positions travel as numeric columns, so `atomium` pins them with `{expr}` islands, one per centre, comma-separated inside one chain group:

```bash
sapia run atomium <run_dir> -t <table> -i <structure_col> \
    --chains-to-design D \
    --fixed-positions '{hisbond_af3_centre1_idx1},{hisbond_af3_centre2_idx1}'
```

Use **`idx1`, never `resnum`** (atomium renumbers every chain to 1..L), with the leaf-prefixed names of the hisbond run that scored that structure. `--fixed-positions` requires `--chains-to-design`, and its `/` groups map one-to-one onto those chains. **The join key back to the contact cells is `centre<k>_idx1` itself**, with no arithmetic: every `per_centre*` entry is prefixed with that same 1-based position. (Before 2026-10-20 the prefixes were 0-based and the key was `centre<k>_idx1 - 1`; a leaf collected under the old build is NOT comparable and must be re-run with `--force`.)

An integral float (`12.0`, which is what these columns are in any real table) resolves to the int `12`; a non-integral value, NaN or a missing cell raises. **Arithmetic works only inside the braces** (`'{c1+1}'` works, `'{c1}+1'` fails with `non-integer position '12+1'`).

**THE TRAP: an empty cell does not stop the lookup — on THIS hand-off.** `ctx.lookup` falls through a NaN own cell to the parent row and returns the first non-NaN value up the lineage (and does the same when the column is absent from the row's own table but present in an ancestor), so an island can pin ANOTHER design's histidine under this design's name with no error. **hisbond's own `--centers` is immune (it stops at the first table that HAS the column — see the premise), but `atomium --fixed-positions` and every other `{expr}` flag are not**, so this guard is still required here. The hand-off is safe only when **(1)** every selected row has a non-NaN own cell: select with `-f` on `hisbond_<leaf>_status == "OK"` and `centers_his_ok == centers_n`, and check that the NaN count of each referenced `centre<k>_idx1` among the selected rows is 0; and **(2)** no ancestor table has a column of the same name (check every ancestor header up to the root).

**`centres_idx1_eq_resnum` does not confirm atomium's numbering**: it compares two numberings inside the CIF. atomium reads a different list: prosapia's `ensure_pdb` converts the CIF (auth chain letters and numbers kept; residues that repeat a number within a chain are not merged; HETATM and both altlocs kept), then ProteinMPNN's / AtomiUM's own PDB parser (not read here) produces the 1..L numbering, which can diverge on a repeated residue number, a HETATM or modified residue, an altloc, or a residue missing backbone atoms. The only real confirmation is atomium's own output: after the run, `atomium_sequence[idx1 - 1] == "H"` for every pinned centre of every design, and `len(atomium_sequence) == hisbond_<leaf>_binder_chain_n_res`.

## Traps, each with its signature

- **A native-binder control errors on every row, and that is correct.** *Signature:* `error: centre position 6 of binder chain D is LEU D6, not a histidine`. The native sequence has no His at the designed centres; do not relax the check, run hisbond on the designs only.
- **A wrong chain letter is caught by luck without `--expect-binder-len-col`.** *Lucky:* `error: centre position 12 of binder chain A is VAL A12, not a histidine`. *Unlucky:* a healthy `OK` row with `binder_chain_n_res` 152 where 44 was expected. Read `binder_chain_n_res` on every batch even with the flag set.
- **A distance that is not an H-bond.** *Signature:* `n_near_target > n_partner_atoms` in `per_centre` and `n_centres_polar_dist_only_target > n_centres_hb_target`. That is the proxy working, and the number to quote when someone says "the His is 2.6 A from the target".
- **A clash is not a bond, and `min_dist_target` alone will fool you.** *Signature:* `min_dist_target` under ~2.4 A with `n_centres_hb_target == 0` and `n_centres_too_close_target == 1`. Never quote `min_dist_target` as evidence of a bond.
- **The centre positions are the same on every child row of one parent.** The rows differ (each is its own prediction) while the position columns the islands read do not.
- **An `acid_p` / `all_p` atomiumph table errors on every row.** *Signature (CHANGED):* `error: centre position 12 of binder chain D is GLU D12, not a histidine -- either the positions do not map onto this structure, or they are not His centres at all (an acid_p / all_p design pins Asp/Glu and is out of scope here)`. The old token-vocabulary signature (`centre 11:GLU-P is not a histidine token`) is gone: the residue is now read off the structure, so the refusal is the same but the reason and the message are not.
- **Empty cells round-trip as `NaN`**: never filter one with `!= ""`; use `.notna()`. Bool-with-NA columns can arrive as the strings `"True"`/`"False"` (`bool("False")` is True): use `_flag`.
- **`--output-scope all` upstream makes `--expect-binder-len-col` unusable.** atomiumph then joins every chain with `/` and `atomiumph_seq_len` is the whole complex. *Signature:* every row errors with `binder chain D ... has 60 residues but atomiumph_seq_len says 512` (length form) or `... neither an integer length nor a plain one-letter sequence` (sequence form). A refusal, not a wrong number: point the flag at a genuine binder length or drop it.
- **An integer column arrives as `"60.0"`** once any row is NA (pandas makes it float64). The parser accepts integral floats and refuses `60.5`. *Signature if this regresses:* every design errors on the length check at once.
- **A relaxed-PDB leaf has no pLDDT, and a confidence clause on it empties the frame.** See the filters section.
- **An island that resolves to nothing errors that row, it does not shorten the list, and it does not borrow the parent's value.** *Signature:* at submit, `<design>: could not resolve --centers '...': design '<design>': column 'atomiumph_centre2_idx1' not found (or empty) in lineage -- submitting anyway so the task records an error`, then a `hisbond_status` of `error: could not resolve --centers ...` with `centers_spec` NA. **It fires even when an ancestor row HAS a value** — that is the point. A row with one centre measured against a two-centre template would otherwise look like a design that lost a bond, or worse, like one that kept a bond it never had. Split the leaf by centre count (`-f`) if the table really is heterogeneous.
- **Positions resolved from an ancestor table are legitimate but must be looked at.** *Signature:* at submit, `hisbond: N/M design(s) resolved at least one --centers island from an ANCESTOR table ...`, and `hisbond_centers_lineage_depth > 0` on the collected rows. Normal when hisbond runs on a Boltz/AF3 grandchild of the atomiumph row (the structure table never carries the centre columns). **Check that each row's ancestor at that depth really is its own design**, i.e. that the lineage is one atomiumph row per branch, not a merge.
- **If EVERY design fails to resolve `--centers`, the submit is REFUSED** rather than filling the run with error rows. *Signature:* `hisbond resolved --centers '...' for NONE of the N ready designs ... First failure: ...`. That is a typo in the flag or a wrong `-t`.
- **Islands are resolved by the lineage, so a comma-joined cell cannot ride through one.** *Signature:* `design 'x': column 'atomiumph_centers'='5:HIS-S;10:HIS-S' is not an integer`, from prosapia's own `_resolve_column`. Name one numeric column per centre.
- **A duplicate position is refused, at submit and in the worker.** *Signature:* `centre position(s) [12] appear twice in '12,12'`. Two islands resolving to the same column is the usual cause.
- **`none` tautomer, HIP and foreign H** are file problems, not results. `none`: *signature* `n_centres_no_ring_h >= 1`, `role_basis` `mixed` (or `partner_class` when every centre is like that), a stderr `WARNING: histidine ... carries no hydrogen within 1.2 A`; the tool falls back to the partner class instead of inventing acceptor roles. **One rule: `n_centres_no_ring_h == 0` is a clause of the pH filter and a failing row is excluded, not interpreted.** HIP: *signature* `n_contacts_incoherent_target > 0` on rows with `n_centres_hip > 0`; report the two together. Foreign H: *signature* `n_centres_foreign_h >= 1` with a stderr `WARNING: histidine ... of ANOTHER residue within 1.2 A`; read it as "something is jammed against that ring" and check `n_centres_too_close_*`. Grep the task logs for `WARNING: histidine` on any new structure source.
- **A role call rests on one structure.** In a Rosetta pose the tautomer is the energy function's choice for that pose, and a second relax or another predictor can flip it and every role on that centre. **Require a switch-candidate call to repeat across two independent structures before spending on it**; a tautomer that flips between leaves is undetermined, not a contradiction.
- **A `:none` placeholder is not an entry.** *Signature of the mistake:* summing `acc+don+amb+incoh+unclass` over a centre and expecting the number of `;`-separated items in the two per-nitrogen cells. A centre with no qualifying contact contributes one placeholder per cell and zero entries. The `;` inside a per-nitrogen cell separates ENTRIES, not centres.
- **A leaf run before a column family existed lacks it, and re-collecting adds nothing.** A plain re-collect leaves already-`OK` rows alone; `collect --force` turns them into `error: OK row missing per_centre_nd1_target,per_centre_role_target,...` (the completeness check working, not a crash). **Re-RUN the leaf (`--force`); old leaves are re-run, not patched.**
- **A corrected run needs `--force` or a new `-l`**: rows already `OK` are skipped. The builder deletes each ready design's stale `<name>.tsv` only after every refusal guard has passed, so a refused submit leaves earlier results intact and a task killed afterwards collects as `missing`, never as the previous `OK`.
- **Submit proof per executor** (`Submitting N designs`, `<script>_modal.json` on Modal, job IDs on vib) is in `running-a-step`; an exit code proves nothing.

**Where the rule is narrower than the sentence describing it** (none observed to bite on Boltz output, each would bite elsewhere):

- The i-1 / i+1 backbone exclusion is **list adjacency**, not residue-number adjacency: across a chain break or a numbering jump the excluded "neighbour" is the next residue listed, and a genuinely bonded neighbour separated in the list is kept.
- `plddt_scale` is inferred from the **whole file's** largest B-factor, so a stitched file mixing conventions between chains gets one verdict.
- Microheterogeneity altlocs can split a residue: the residue key is `(resname, seq, icode)`, so altlocs disagreeing on the residue name are two residues and shift every later centre index (impossible in a Boltz/AF prediction, possible in a crystal structure).
- "A hydrogen of the centre's own residue" is keyed on `(chain, resnum, icode)` **without the residue name**: on a chain whose numbers repeat (symmetric fused protomers, an un-split rfd3 chain) or with altlocs disagreeing on the name, a neighbour's H within 1.2 A could be taken as the centre's own and set the tautomer instead of counting in `n_centres_foreign_h`.
- A duplicated position in `--centers` is REFUSED, at submit and in the worker (`centre position(s) [12] appear twice in '12,12'`), so a His can no longer be double-counted in an aggregate.

## Invariants a worker can check

**Real checks, from the raw CIF/PDB** (hisbond computes none of these):

1. The binder chain read one letter per residue in file order has length `hisbond_binder_chain_n_res` and the parent table's binder sequence length.
2. The residue at position `p` of that sequence (1-based, so `sequence[p - 1]`) is `H` for every `p` in `hisbond_centers_spec`.
3. `hisbond_centers_n` equals the number of positions in `hisbond_centers_spec`, equals the number of `{expr}` islands in `hisbond_centers_arg`, and equals the centre count of the tool that produced the positions (e.g. `atomiumph_n_centers`). **The last comparison is the non-tautological one**: it is the only thing that catches islands pointing at the wrong parent.
3b. **Where the positions came from, against the table headers.** `hisbond_centers_lineage_depth == 0` is exact: **every** column named in `hisbond_centers_arg` must be present in this table's own header. A depth of `d > 0` constrains only the **deepest** island — at least one named column is absent here and first appears `d` steps up `parent_table`, while the others may sit anywhere from 0 to `d`; **check each named column separately**, do not read one depth as a statement about all of them (it only coincides when every island names the same producer, which is the usual case). Walk the steps by hand on one row and confirm each ancestor row is that design's own ancestor and its `centre<k>_idx1` equals the matching entry of `hisbond_centers_spec`. **This is the check that catches a merge lifting the column onto two generations**, which no in-tool column can see.
4. **The check that proves the partner labels, before trusting any ND1 column.** Take one `OK` row with `n_centres_hb_target_nd1 >= 1` and one entry `<idx>:<chain>:<resnum>:<resname>:<atom>=<d>/<a>`. From the raw file: that atom exists (auth chain, residue number, residue name, atom name all match), its chain is one of `--target-chains` and is not `--binder-chain`; `|ND1 - partner|` for the `<idx1>`-th binder residue in file order (1-based, so `binder_residues[idx1 - 1]`) equals `<d>` to 0.01 A; the angle between `-(u(ND1->CG) + u(ND1->CE1))` and `ND1->partner` equals `<a>` to 0.1 deg; the distinct atoms across that centre's ND1 and NE2 cells equal its `n_partner_atoms` in `per_centre`. This catches a wrong chain namespace, an off-by-one centre index and a swapped nitrogen in one pass.
5. External confirmation of `idx1`: the `centre<k>_idx1`-th residue of the binder chain in the raw file is a histidine with `auth_seq_id == centre<k>_resnum`, and atomium's own output agrees (see the hand-off section).
6. **Role reading against an independent reader** (your own parser, not a re-run of the tool): on 10 `OK` rows of a hydrogen-bearing leaf, whether an H/D sits within 1.2 A of ND1 and of NE2 must match `centre<k>_tautomer` exactly; classify each per-nitrogen partner by hand from the rules table and the roles must match the counts. Pick one centre, list the target ASP/GLU/LYS/ARG residues within `env_radius` of the ring centroid, and check the env column equals their listed **atoms** (Asp 2, Glu 2, Lys 1, Arg 3) with no binder residue counted.

**Real checks, from the table alone** (two independent paths must agree):

7. `n_centres_polar_dist_only_target >= n_centres_hb_target` (the cone only removes atoms); `n_centres_hb_target <= centers_n`; `n_centres_polar_dist_only_target >= 1` implies `min_dist_target <= max_dist`, and `min_dist_target > max_dist` implies a distance-only count of 0. **Only those implications hold**: a nearest partner under the floor gives a small `min_dist_target` with a distance-only count of 0, which is correct.
8. Per centre, the union of atoms in `per_centre_nd1_target` and `per_centre_ne2_target` equals that centre's merged `n_partner_atoms` in `per_centre` (an atom in both cells counts once; dedupe by file position, not printed label, if a target chain repeats a residue number). `max(nd1, ne2) <= n_centres_hb_target <= nd1 + ne2`, the upper bound usually strict. Every position of `hisbond_centers_spec` appears as a prefix in both per-nitrogen cells (a hit or `:none`), and every entry's chain is one of `--target-chains`.
9. **The partition**, per centre and over the batch: `acc + don + amb + incoh + unclass` in `per_centre_role_target` equals the `=`-bearing entries that centre contributes to the two per-nitrogen cells; the row-level accounting identity holds per row, not only in aggregate. A mismatch makes everything downstream unusable.
10. **Binder side**: the number of centres whose `per_centre_role_binder` counts sum above 0 equals `n_centres_hb_binder`; and `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_any` recomputes from `per_centre_role_target` + `per_centre_role_binder` (per centre: `acc_t + acc_b > 0 and don_t + don_b == 0 and incoh_t + incoh_b == 0`), the only check that the cross-side rule is the stated one.
11. Defect counters are `0` on every row of a healthy relaxed leaf; each non-zero row is a finding to report (design name plus the stderr `WARNING: histidine` line), and `n_centres_no_ring_h > 0` must come with `role_basis` `mixed` or `partner_class` on that row, never `tautomer` (the check that the fallback happened). A hydrogen-free leaf must give `role_basis == partner_class`, every `centre<k>_tautomer` NA, `n_contacts_incoherent_target == 0` and `amb > 0` on some centres.

**Tautologies** (pass by construction, prove nothing; do not report them as checks): `any_target_hb_proxy == (n_centres_hb_target >= 1)` and the same for `_nd1`, `_ne2` and `any_switch_candidate`; `min_dist_target == min(min_dist_target_nd1, min_dist_target_ne2)`; `centre<k>_idx1 ==` the k-th value of `hisbond_centers_spec` (and of `--centers`); `centers_his_ok == centers_n` on an `OK` row (keep it in a filter anyway, it excludes the error rows whose count stopped short); the five counts of `per_centre_role_binder` summing to `n_contacts_binder_entries`; `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean <= n_centres_switch_candidate <= n_centres_acceptor_role_target <= n_centres_hb_target`; `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_any <= centers_n`; `n_contacts_incoherent_target == 0` (and `_binder`) in `partner_class` mode.

**Break-it controls**, each under its own `-l`, never `verification`:

1. Run the native-binder structure column (no His at the designed positions): every row must be `error: centre position ... not a histidine` with every science column NA. A number, or a 0, means the mapping check is broken and nothing else here can be trusted.
2. Strip the hydrogens from one relaxed PDB and re-run the same designs: contact columns must be **identical** to the hydrogen-bearing leaf, while `role_basis` flips to `partner_class`, every `centre<k>_tautomer` goes NA and `n_contacts_incoherent_target` goes 0. A contact column that moves means a hydrogen leaked into the geometry.
3. `--env-radius 0` must be refused at submit (`--env-radius must be a positive number of angstroms`).
4. A target with no charged residues (or `--env-radius 1.0`) must give `n_acidic_env == 0` and `n_basic_env == 0` on an `OK` row, a measurement and not NA.
5. Move one centre's ring hydrogen out to 1.5 A: that centre must read `none`, `n_centres_no_ring_h == 1`, `role_basis` `mixed`, and its acceptor-role count must not grow. A rise means the fallback is not wired.
6. Add a hydrogen of a neighbouring residue 1.0 A from a ring nitrogen: `n_centres_foreign_h` becomes 1 and `centre<k>_tautomer` must not change.
7. `--centers '0'` and `--centers '12,12'` must both be refused at submit, and `--centers '{a_column_that_is_NA_on_some_rows}'` must give those rows `error: could not resolve --centers ...` while the others stay `OK`. An off-by-one control: re-run one design with every position shifted by 1 (`--centers '{c1+1}'`) under its own `-l` -- it must error with `not a histidine` on a binder that has no adjacent His pair.
8. **The fall-through control, the one that matters.** Take a table whose own `centre2_idx1` is NA on some rows while the PARENT table has a value for those rows' parents. Every such row must come back `error: could not resolve --centers ...`, and **not** `OK` with a second centre. If any is `OK`, the lookup has reverted to `ctx.lookup` and every two-centre number in the leaf is suspect.
9. **The His-variant control, in two halves.** (a) *Neutral spellings:* run a relaxed PDB whose histidines are written `HID`/`HIE`/`HSD`/`HSE` and one whose are written `HIS`, same designs, two `-l` labels. `n_centres_acceptor_role_binder`, `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_binder` and `_clean_any` must be IDENTICAL; `n_contacts_unclassified_binder` must not be higher on the variant-name leaf. (b) *HIP:* rename one designed-His PARTNER (a `*`-marked residue) to `HIP` and re-run. At a centre whose own protonated nitrogen reaches it, `n_contacts_incoherent_binder` must RISE by that contact and `n_centres_switch_candidate_clean_binder` must not rise — the opposite direction from (a). **If (b) shows no change, HIP is being folded into HIS and the clean gates are reading false-clean.**

## What it is blind to

- **The tautomer on a hydrogen-free file.** On a CIF the bisector is where an H or a lone pair points and the tool cannot tell which, so roles rest on the partner's chemistry (`partner_class`) and a both-capable partner stays ambiguous. On a hydrogen-bearing file the tautomer is read, but in a Rosetta pose it is the energy function's choice, not a measured state. Which nitrogen donates is an inference from lookups in both modes. A real pKa needs a dedicated program (`propka`, `reduce`/`pdb2pqr`, or a constant-pH model).
- **The donor's hydrogen geometry.** H...acceptor distance and D-H...A angle are never computed, only which nitrogen carries the H: a donor-role contact can have its hydrogen pointing 90 deg away.
- **A ring flip.** A 180 deg rotation about CB-CG swaps which nitrogen faces the partner, and the tautomer label follows names, not physics; a surface His's chi2 is often the least determined thing in the side chain. Nothing here detects it (`his_plddt_min` is per residue, not per torsion). **Before any spend that turns on ND1 specifically, require the ND1 hit to repeat across independent predictions** and report the `(nd1, ne2)` pair, never ND1 alone. The role split inherits this blindness, on the binder side too.
- **A His partner's own protonation — EXCEPT when the file names it.** A partner written `HIS` (or the neutral spellings `HID`/`HIE`/`HSD`/`HSE`, which are folded into it) is both-capable and its tautomer is NOT read, so two designed histidines bonded to each other can both be reported acceptor-role although only one can be. That inflates acceptor counts, and the `*` in `per_centre_nd1_binder` / `_ne2_binder` is how you see it: look at it first on any binder-side candidate. **A partner written `HIP`/`HSP` is the exception and IS read**: a doubly protonated ring carries an H on both nitrogens, so it is donor-only at ND1 and NE2, and a contact to it at a centre's own protonated nitrogen scores `incoh` rather than `don`. A relaxer that writes everything `HIS` therefore leaves this blindness in place; one that names HIP removes it for those partners only. Read `structure_has_hydrogens` and the partner resnames in the per-nitrogen cells before assuming either.
- **The partner's own geometry.** The cone is one-sided (the angle at the His ring only), so a contact can pass while the partner's lone pair or H faces the wrong way.
- **A pKa, a pKa shift and pH-dependent affinity.** The env counts are raw atom counts in a sphere; `n_centres_switch_candidate` says a contact is of the kind acid breaks, with no magnitude and no pH. Whether the contact is pH-dependent at all is the design claim (`atomiumph_ddE_mean`, `ddE_min`), a model log-probability and not a titration. Burial: use propka's `buried_free`.
- **Bonds through a water or to a residue the predictor did not model.** Neither is in the file, and a real recycling switch may well be water-mediated. Sulfur acceptors (Met SD) read as no contact.
- **Which atoms sit only inside the window or under the floor, and every binder-side atom.** Qualifying target atoms are named per nitrogen and qualifying binder atoms in the binder cells, but near-window atoms (`n_near_*`) and clashes (`n_too_close_*`) are only counted, never named.
- **Whether there is an interface worth the name**: `cms`, `pyrosetta` (`if_dG`, `if_dSASA`, `if_delta_unsat`), `epitope`.
- **Whether the His is where the predictor thinks.** `his_plddt_min` is the predictor's own belief; the pose gate is `boltzconf` and `usalign`.
- **Anything about the sequence and its expression.** This is geometry on one pose.
